# SavedMessages

Save messages locally so you can find and jump back to them later.

- Right-click any message and choose **Save Message** to store it.
- Click the 📌 icon in the chat bar to open your saved messages.
- Each entry shows the author, server/channel, and timestamp, and lets you jump straight to the original message or remove it from your list.

Saved messages are stored locally on your device and are not synced anywhere.
